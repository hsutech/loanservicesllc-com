05 · Education

Education loans.

Understand the payment before you sign the note.

What it covers

Undergraduate gap funding, graduate programs, professional certifications and bar or board review costs.

Who this fits

Students and parents who want to understand payment fit before signing a private education loan.

Method

How we prepare this file.

01

Gap

We work out the actual funding gap after federal aid, scholarships and family contribution.

02

Terms

We model accrual, grace period and post-graduation payment on realistic starting salaries.

03

Cosigner

If a cosigner is involved, we explain release conditions in plain language before the note is signed.

Documents

What you will typically need.

  • 01 · Enrollment or acceptance letter
  • 02 · Cost-of-attendance breakdown
  • 03 · Cosigner information if applicable
  • 04 · Existing aid summary

Questions

Federal first?

Almost always. We help you sequence federal, institutional and private borrowing in that order.

Do you help with refinancing student loans?

Yes, once income is stable, refinancing can become the best next move.
