About the firm

A quiet room

Loan Services LLC is a small, Palm Beach–based practice for borrowers who want a considered second read before they apply anywhere.

We help you understand what you're really asking for.

Most confusion around a loan doesn't come from math. It comes from a request that hasn't been named clearly — the purpose is fuzzy, the documents are stale, and the payment hasn't been placed against real monthly obligations.

Our work is to name the request, put the documents in one place, and stress-test the payment before it becomes an application. When a lender does see your file, it looks like it was prepared by someone who understood the question.

We serve individuals, families and small owner-operated businesses across South Florida. We don't sell loans and we don't earn a spread from a lender. We charge a straightforward preparation fee, disclosed up front.

Principles

Three, only
I

Preparation over urgency

A loan request that arrives organized is reviewed differently than one that arrives late and thin. We work on the file, not the deadline.

II

Documentation over promises

We don't promise approval. What we can promise is that your file will actually reflect your income, obligations and purpose in a form a lender expects.

III

Fit over speed

The fastest loan is not always the right loan. Payment fit and term structure matter more than a two-day turnaround that costs you later.
