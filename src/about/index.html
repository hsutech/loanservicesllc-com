About the firm

A quiet room
for a loud
decision.

Loan Services LLC is a small Palm Beach based practice for borrowers who want a considered second read before they apply anywhere.

We help you understand what you are really asking for.

Most confusion around a loan does not come from math. It comes from a request that has not been named clearly: the purpose is fuzzy, the documents are stale and the payment has not been placed against real monthly obligations.

Our work is to name the request, put the documents in one place and stress-test the payment before it becomes an application. When a lender does see your file, it looks like it was prepared by someone who understood the question.

We serve individuals, families and small owner-operated businesses across South Florida. We do not sell loans and we do not earn a spread from a lender. We charge a straightforward preparation fee, disclosed up front.

Principles

Three, only
I

Preparation over urgency

A loan request that arrives organized is reviewed differently than one that arrives late and thin.

II

Documentation over promises

We do not promise approval. We can promise that your file will reflect income, obligations and purpose clearly.

III

Fit over speed

The fastest loan is not always the right loan. Payment fit and term structure matter more than a two-day turnaround that costs you later.
