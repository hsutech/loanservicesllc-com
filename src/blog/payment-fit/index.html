Understanding payment fit before you sign a term sheet

Loan Services LLC · February 2, 2026

Contents

The first readRelated services

Most borrowers evaluate a loan as a monthly number. Most of the time, that number technically fits inside a ratio or spreadsheet. What it often does not fit inside is the actual shape of a month.

The first read

A payment that fits on paper assumes an average month. A payment that fits in reality has to survive months that are not average. Payment fit asks whether the new line item survives the third-worst month of the year.

Preparation changes the reading experience before pricing is even discussed.

Print the last twelve months of your primary checking account. Circle the three months with the lowest ending balances. Add the proposed payment to those months, retroactively.

If those months still end above zero without leaning on a credit card or savings account you did not want to touch, the payment fits. If not, negotiate structure before signing.
