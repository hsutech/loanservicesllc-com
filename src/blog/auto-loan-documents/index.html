Preparing documents for an auto loan before you visit the dealer

Loan Services LLC · February 19, 2026

Contents

The first readRelated services

The finance desk at a dealership is designed for speed. Speed is not the environment in which to first assemble your income documents, hunt for a utility bill, or decide how much down payment you can actually part with.

Before the desk

A workable pre-visit file is short: two recent pay stubs, proof of address, photo ID, a note on the target vehicle and the number you intend to put down.

Preparation changes the reading experience before pricing is even discussed.

Bring one more thing: an outside pre-approval. It does not need to beat the dealership offer. It only needs to exist, because a real number from a real lender changes the conversation.

Finally, read the contract: rate, term, add-ons and prepayment terms. If any of them differ from what you agreed to verbally, stop. Prepared buyers are allowed to stop.
