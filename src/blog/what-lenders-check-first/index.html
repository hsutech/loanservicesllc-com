What lenders actually check first

Loan Services LLC · March 4, 2026

Contents

The first readRelated services

It is tempting to think a loan file is read the way a resume is read: from the top, in order, with the headline doing the heavy lifting. It is not. An underwriter first pass is short, tactical and usually focused on the same signals.

The first read

The first is the pattern of deposits. Regular, similar-sized deposits on predictable dates tell a story that a single large deposit cannot. If income arrives on the 1st and 15th, an underwriter can model it quickly.

Preparation changes the reading experience before pricing is even discussed.

The second is the ending balance across the last two months, not the highest balance. Underwriters look for the number the account rests at. A cash account that ends every month within a narrow band is treated differently from one that swings widely.

The third is the presence or absence of other lender debits. A recurring line item to a consumer lender, buy-now-pay-later service or cash advance changes the shape of the request. It is better to name that obligation than let it appear unexplained.
