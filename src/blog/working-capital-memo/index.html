A working capital request that reads like a plan, not a plea

Loan Services LLC · January 6, 2026

Contents

The first readRelated services

A working capital request lives or dies on its first page. Credit officers read many of these files, and a request without a cover memo forces the reader to assemble the story alone.

The first read

A good memo has four parts: the ask, the use of funds, the revenue line that supports repayment and a short paragraph on the operator.

Preparation changes the reading experience before pricing is even discussed.

That last paragraph is the one most owners skip. A borrower with a plan for not getting the loan is a different reading experience from one assuming approval.

The memo does not replace the numbers. It orders them: returns, P&L, statements and debt schedule behind a clear cover page.
