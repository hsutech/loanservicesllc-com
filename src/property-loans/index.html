03 · Property

Property loans.

A real file, built before you shop rates.

What it covers

Primary residence, second home, small investment property, and equity requests against an owned home.

Who this fits

Palm Beach and South Florida borrowers who want a real file organized before shopping rates.

Method

How we prepare this file.

01

Scenario

Primary, second home, investment, or equity: each one is underwritten differently. We pick the right lane first.

02

Package

Returns, statements, insurance, tax and HOA figures assembled into a lender-ready folder.

03

Comparison

We help you read real offers side by side: rate, points, cash to close and monthly payment.

Documents

What you will typically need.

  • 01 · Two years of tax returns
  • 02 · Two months of bank statements
  • 03 · Homeowners insurance quote
  • 04 · Property tax and HOA figures

Questions

Do you originate mortgages?

No. We prepare your file and help you compare offers. Origination stays with the lender.

How far in advance should I start?

Ninety days before you plan to make an offer is a comfortable window. Sixty is workable. Thirty is tight.
