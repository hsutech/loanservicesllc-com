04 · Business

Business loans.

A one-page memo beats a rushed application.

What it covers

Working capital, equipment purchase, expansion, and acquisition financing for owner-operated companies.

Who this fits

Owners who need a lender-ready package instead of a rushed application from a broker cold call.

Method

How we prepare this file.

01

Ask

We frame the request with a specific use of funds and the revenue line it supports.

02

Numbers

Two years of returns, a clean YTD P&L, six months of business statements and a current debt schedule.

03

Memo

A one-page cover memo that a credit officer can read before opening the exhibits.

Documents

What you will typically need.

  • 01 · Business tax returns, 2 years
  • 02 · Year-to-date P&L and balance sheet
  • 03 · Business bank statements, 6 months
  • 04 · Debt schedule

Questions

Do you work with startups?

We work with operating companies that have at least a full year of revenue history.

SBA or conventional?

Both, depending on the ask and the timeline. We help you decide which lane fits before you apply.
