Services

Six categories, one method.

The categories change; the preparation does not. In each area we help you decide what you are really asking for, gather what a lender expects and check whether the payment fits before anything is submitted.

01 · Personal

Personal loans

What it covers

Consolidation, medical expenses, moving costs, family support, planned major purchases.

Who this fits

Salaried or self-employed borrowers who want a clean unsecured request with a clear repayment story.

Documents typically needed

  • Photo ID
  • Two most recent pay stubs
  • Last two months of bank statements
  • List of current monthly obligations
Read the brief →
02 · Automotive

Auto loans

What it covers

New or used vehicle purchase, refinance of an existing auto loan, private-party purchase paperwork.

Who this fits

Buyers comparing dealer financing with an outside lender before signing at the desk.

Documents typically needed

  • Vehicle details or VIN
  • Down payment source
  • Insurance quote
  • Proof of income and residence
Read the brief →
03 · Property

Property loans

What it covers

Primary residence, second home, small investment property, and equity requests against an owned home.

Who this fits

Palm Beach and South Florida borrowers who want a real file organized before shopping rates.

Documents typically needed

  • Two years of tax returns
  • Two months of bank statements
  • Homeowners insurance quote
  • Property tax and HOA figures
Read the brief →
04 · Business

Business loans

What it covers

Working capital, equipment purchase, expansion, and acquisition financing for owner-operated companies.

Who this fits

Owners who need a lender-ready package instead of a rushed application from a broker cold call.

Documents typically needed

  • Business tax returns, 2 years
  • Year-to-date P&L and balance sheet
  • Business bank statements, 6 months
  • Debt schedule
Read the brief →
05 · Education

Education loans

What it covers

Undergraduate gap funding, graduate programs, professional certifications and bar or board review costs.

Who this fits

Students and parents who want to understand payment fit before signing a private education loan.

Documents typically needed

  • Enrollment or acceptance letter
  • Cost-of-attendance breakdown
  • Cosigner information if applicable
  • Existing aid summary
Read the brief →
06 · Wedding

Wedding loans

What it covers

Venue, catering, travel and vendor deposits for planned celebrations with fixed timelines.

Who this fits

Couples who want a written plan instead of stacking credit cards through the planning window.

Documents typically needed

  • Vendor estimates and deposit schedule
  • Income verification
  • Existing revolving balances
  • Target payoff horizon
