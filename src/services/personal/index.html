01 · Personal

Personal loans.

An unsecured request, organized like a file — not a form.

What it covers

Consolidation, medical expenses, moving costs, family support, planned major purchases.

Who this fits

Salaried or self-employed borrowers who want a clean unsecured request with a clear repayment story.

Method

How we prepare this file.

01

Purpose

We write down what the money is for in one sentence. If it takes more than one, we split the request.

02

Capacity

We compare the proposed payment against your last two months of real cash flow, not a monthly average.

03

File

We assemble ID, income and obligations into a single package a lender can read in under five minutes.

Documents

What you'll typically need.

  • 01Photo ID
  • 02Two most recent pay stubs
  • 03Last two months of bank statements
  • 04List of current monthly obligations

Questions

Do you lend the money?

No. We prepare the request and guide the process. Funding comes from the lender you choose.

Will you pull my credit?

Not at the preparation stage. We work from a self-reported obligations list until you're ready to submit.
